ESP32 + LDR VOLTAGE DIVIDER

             LDR
3.3V --------/\/\--------+-------- GPIO 34 (ADC)
                         |
                       10 kΩ
                         |
GND ---------------------+

Connections:
LDR terminal 1 -> ESP32 3.3V
LDR terminal 2 -> ESP32 GPIO 34
10 kΩ resistor terminal 1 -> GPIO 34
10 kΩ resistor terminal 2 -> ESP32 GND

GPIO 34 is used as the analog input.
